Context

Building fintech in Laos means working with local payment rails, bank partnerships, and regulatory expectations around identity and KYC. Mobile-first users expect LaoQR and familiar banking flows.

Mobile banking APIs

Bank APIs differ in auth (OAuth, API keys, mutual TLS) and sandbox availability. Start with a thin adapter layer so you can swap providers without rewriting business logic.

eKYC in production

Electronic KYC combines document capture, liveness checks, and vendor verification. Plan for retries, poor network, and manual review queues.

Lessons learned

  • Design for intermittent connectivity
  • Localize copy and error messages (Lao + English)
  • Instrument every payment step for ops dashboards
  • Ship small integrations before bundling the full stack
